# PRODUCT.md — Multi-day bike trip briefs

## Audience
Pietro and a riding partner. They decide overnight towns, loads GPX on devices, and share one link. They are busy; the brief must work on first read on a phone.

## Job of the page
Operate: scan days, distances, overnight, map, and downloads in under a minute. Persuade only enough that the partner trusts the plan without a call.

## Must show
- Dates, start/end, day table (km, climb, overnight)
- Interactive map of real corridor geometry (not town-to-town lines)
- Lodging shortlist with live-checked notes; never pretend a booking exists
- GPX downloads for each day and full loop
- Hard constraints: public roads only, ~70 km days when possible, REM/car note when Montreal start

## Voice
Full clear sentences. Lead with the useful point. No em dashes, no emoji, no cute compressed labels, no assistant rules dumped into rider-facing copy.

## Anti-references
Generic “AI travel blog” hero with stock mountains, rainbow gradients, icon+title+paragraph card grids as the whole page, fake testimonials, fake “booked” badges.
