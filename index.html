Track sources

  • Day 1 (~75.8 km): Gravelee (bikepacking.com Eastern Townships / stage 1) public GPX, cut short into Waterloo; short OSRM bike spur to Les Matins de Victoria. Private-road segments from the original file were replaced with public-road OSRM/BRouter alternatives.
  • Day 2 (~78.5 km): Custom route from Les Matins de Victoria: BRouter gravel to Parc national de la Yamaska, then an ~18 km lake circuit on OSM bike-legal park cycleways (Piste Nord / Piste Sud), then BRouter gravel south through Knowlton / Lac-Brome to Mont Sutton. Skips Bromont. Not the official Gravelee Day 2 (that goes to Racine). Scrubbed off bicycle=no paths.

Access

Both days were checked against OpenStreetMap for ways tagged access=private, bicycle=no, or access=no (8 m on-track threshold). The published tracks stay on named public roads, bike paths, or other ways open to cycling. Day 2 uses park cycleways tagged bicycle=designated (Piste Nord / Piste Sud) for the lake loop; hiking paths tagged bicycle=no were avoided. A Knowlton-area bicycle=no path and the Sentier Village-Montagne trailhead near Mont Sutton were scrubbed onto public roads. Private driveway stubs within 8 m of the track are proximity only, not ridden. The short hotel driveway at Les Matins de Victoria in Waterloo is guest access only.

  • Day 2 is about 78.5 km: Yamaska lake loop on park cycleways, then quieter roads through Knowlton to Mont Sutton. Lunch in Knowlton, then finish and drive home. Sepaq day access applies for the park loop.
