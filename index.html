Day 1 · Mon 21 · Sutton → Granby

Drive Outremont → Mont Sutton (~1 h 50). Roll by ~8:00. Follow Gravelée stage 1 toward Yamaska, then peel into Granby for the hotel instead of continuing to the national park.

  • Food: N-Latte in Bromont.
  • Skip Yamaska camping.
  • Arrive Granby mid-afternoon.

Day 2 · Tue 22 · Granby → Sutton

Roll by 7:30–8:00. Estriade toward Waterloo, then Bromont corridor, Lac-Brome / Knowlton, into Mont Sutton. Finish, load the car, drive home (~1 h 50–2 h).

  • Estriade open year-round 6:00–22:00; no Sep 2026 closure found on last check.
  • Public roads / path only.
