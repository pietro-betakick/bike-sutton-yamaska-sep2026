Overnight

Les Matins de Victoria · Waterloo

Primary lodging. Small-town inn. Live-checked for Mon 21 to Tue 22 Sep 2026, two adults, one room. Park-edge hotels and motels closer to Yamaska were checked the same day; nothing hotel-style was available for these dates closer than Waterloo. Granby Motel Bonsoir is dropped (off-track west of the park).

  • Suite with 1 twin + 1 queen · about CAD 184 / night before tax · private bath (prefer)
  • Room with 2 queens · about CAD 154 / night before tax · shared bath

Prefer the suite for separate beds and a private bath. Confirm bath type and ask about bike storage when booking. Nothing is booked yet.

Lunch

Open hours below are from public listings. Mid-late September weekday hours can shift. Check Mon/Tue morning-of by phone or Google.

Day 1 · Bromont corridor ~km 39

  • n latte Bromont ~km 39.1 · 840 Rue Shefford · primary pick Specialty coffee and real lunch (sandwiches, salads, bowls). Listed open Mon-Sun about 07:30-16:00.
  • Confiserie Bromont / Musee du chocolat ~km 39.3 · 679 Rue Shefford · backup Bistro lunch vibe plus pastry. Good if n latte is packed. Confirm lunch service Mon.
  • Cafe 1792 ~km 39.2 · 715 Rue Shefford · backup / short stop Bagels, pastry, soup energy. Safer as a cafe stop than primary lunch unless hours confirmed.

Day 2 · Knowlton / Lac-Brome ~km 57

Carry a snack for the lake loop. Little reliable on-track lunch between Yamaska park and Knowlton. Eat before the Mont-Echo climbs.

  • Raffields ~km 57.1 · 255 Chemin Knowlton · top Mon/Tue pick Crepes, sandwiches, bakery-cafe. Official hours Mon and Tue 08:00-15:00 (Wed-Thu closed).
  • Bistro LeRelais (Auberge Knowlton) ~km 57.1 · 286 Chemin Knowlton · sit-down Proper bistro plate after the lake. Listed Mon-Tue 11:30-20:00. Bike storage culture at the auberge.

Track sources

  • Day 1 (~75.8 km): Official Gravelee stage 1 (Le Quebec a velo / bikepacking.com Eastern Townships) public GPX, cut short toward Waterloo; short public bike spur to Les Matins de Victoria. Private and bicycle=no clips scrubbed onto public-road alternatives. Lake is not on Day 1.
  • Day 2 (~78.5 km): Custom route from Les Matins de Victoria: BRouter gravel to Parc national de la Yamaska, then an ~14 km lake circuit on OSM bike-legal park cycleways (Piste Nord / Piste Sud only), then BRouter gravel south through Knowlton / Lac-Brome to Mont Sutton. Skips Bromont. Not the official Gravelee Day 2 (that goes to Racine). Scrubbed off bicycle=no paths.

Access

Both days were checked against OpenStreetMap for ways tagged access=private, bicycle=no, or access=no (8 m on-track threshold). The published tracks stay on named public roads, bike paths, or other ways open to cycling. Day 2 uses park cycleways tagged bicycle=designated (Piste Nord / Piste Sud) for the lake loop; hiking paths tagged bicycle=no were avoided. A Knowlton-area bicycle=no path and the Sentier Village-Montagne trailhead near Mont Sutton were scrubbed onto public roads. Private driveway stubs within 8 m of the track are proximity only, not ridden. The short hotel driveway at Les Matins de Victoria in Waterloo is guest access only.
