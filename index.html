Access

Both days were checked against OpenStreetMap for ways tagged access=private, bicycle=no, or access=no (8 m on-track threshold). The published tracks stay on named public roads, bike paths, or other ways open to cycling. Day 2 follows the researched Waterloo to Bromont to Knowlton to Sutton gravel corridor on public ways only. The short hotel driveway at Les Matins de Victoria in Waterloo is guest access only.

Notes

  • Day 2 is about 75 km of quieter gravel and public roads. Lunch in Knowlton, then finish at Mont Sutton and drive home.
