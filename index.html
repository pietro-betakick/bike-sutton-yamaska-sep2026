Official full Gravelée is a 3-day 235 km / ~3100 m loop. This trip uses only stage 1, then a scenic shortcut home instead of Racine.

Day 1 · Mon Sep 21 · Sutton → Yamaska

Morning: Drive to Mont SUTTON, 68 chemin Réal. Aim to ride by 8:00 AM. Confirm overnight parking (P2B often used for multi-day; check resort Sep rules).

SegmentNotes
Sutton countrysideQuiet rural + gravel, rolling Appalachian climbs, farms and forest. Views toward Sutton, Brome, Shefford.
Toward BromontMix of gravel and smoother path sections. Full adventure feel with bags.
Bromont → Yamaska networkMore bike-path / Estriade-adjacent riding into the park.

Stops (Monday reality):

  • Pleasant Hôtel & Café — listed on the official itinerary, but closed to the public Mon (breakfast Wed–Sun). Pick another Sutton coffee / grocery.
  • N-Latte Bromont (840 rue Shefford) — open Mon 7:30–16:00. Best reliable lunch / refill.
  • Cidrerie Autour de la Pomme — tasting mostly Sat–Sun; weekdays by appointment.
  • La Ferme (Shefford) — closed Mon and Tue through mid-Oct.

Arrive camp ~16:00–17:00. Early night. Tuesday is still a proper ride.

Night · Parc national de la Yamaska

Pick: bookable tent siteAvailable Sep 21

Sépaq · Parc national de la Yamaska · 1780 boul. David-Bouchard, Roxton Pond

  • Les Roselins from about CA$39.55 / night (sans services). Also Parulines / Sittelles from ~$44.70. Higher with water+power.
  • Checked live 17 Sep for arrival Mon 21 / depart Tue 22 — sectors showing available. Nothing booked.
  • Park is Bienvenue cyclistes: bike path to camping, showers on main camping.
Alt: Bienvenue cyclistes walk-inNo reservation
  • 4 rustic sites in Les Roselins · CA$11 / adult / night + park access fee · first-come for cyclists.
  • If those are full on arrival, fall back to a booked Roselins / Parulines site.

Water advisory: camping sector water is non-potable (indefinite). Refill at sanitary block 2 (Les Mésanges) and the beach pavilion fill station. Bring extra bottles.

Day 2 · Tue Sep 22 · Yamaska → Sutton

Break camp early. Roll by 7:30–8:00. This is a custom return (not official Gravelée day 2 to Racine): south/east through countryside toward Waterloo / Bromont, then Lac-Brome / Knowlton, then into Sutton hills.

SegmentNotes
Yamaska → Waterloo / BromontAgricultural, lower traffic. Coffee stop ~10:00–10:30.
Bromont → Lac-BromeTrade Granby countryside for Brome / Sutton landscape.
Knowlton lunchLa Knowlton Co. (576 ch. Knowlton) open Tue 11:00–21:00. Major resupply.
Knowlton → SuttonUse Traverse des Townships flavour where practical (~47 km loop / ~33 km unpaved if you add it). Climbing into Sutton. Finish Mont Sutton / village, load car, drive home.

Want closer to 85–95 km on Day 2? Add Stagecoach / Traverse gravel loops after Knowlton rather than the shortest BRouter line.
